1️⃣ The core problem Visitor solves
    “I have a stable object structure, but I want to add NEW OPERATIONS on it without modifying the objects.”

Key point:
    Objects don’t change
    Operations change frequently
    This is the opposite of Strategy.

2️⃣ Real-life analogy (lock it in)
    Think of:
        A hospital
        Patients = elements
        Doctors = visitors
    Patients don’t change when:
        Surgeon visits
        Physician visits
        Insurance auditor visits
        Each visitor performs different operations on the same patient.
