Why exactly-k is hard directly?
    Exactly-k is hard because sliding window requires monotonic conditions, and exactly-k is not monotonic—both expanding and shrinking the window can break the condition. However, ‘at most k’ is monotonic and easy to count. Using the identity exactly(k) = atMost(k) − atMost(k−1), we convert the problem into two easy sliding window problems.”


Sliding window works best for:
    👉 monotonic conditions
    Examples:
        sum ≤ k
        at most k distinct
        at most k odd numbers
        Because when the condition breaks:
        moving left always fixes it
        window size moves in one direction

Even when oddCount equals k, we may still need to shrink because multiple valid subarrays can end at the same right index, and sliding window cannot count them cleanly.
