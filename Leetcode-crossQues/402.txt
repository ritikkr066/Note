Why removing from left is optimal?
    Removing from the left is optimal because digits on the left have higher place value and impact the number more than digits on the right. If a larger digit appears before a smaller digit, keeping it would make the number unnecessarily large. So, whenever a digit is greater than the digit after it, removing that left digit allows the smaller digit to move to a more significant position, producing a smaller number overall. This greedy choice ensures the leftmost digits are minimized first, which guarantees the smallest possible result.
